# Code of Conduct

Orka welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. We want
technical discussion to be rigorous without becoming personal or hostile.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Welcome questions and explain project-specific conventions.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and respect privacy.
- Keep issue and pull-request discussions focused on improving Orka.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's
private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile rather than opening a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made
without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
