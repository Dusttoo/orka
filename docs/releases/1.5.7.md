# Orka 1.5.7

Orka 1.5.7 adds bounded operator recovery for escalated review ledgers while
preserving the evidence and limits that caused the escalation.

## Review escalation recovery

- New ticket workflows bind their review ledger to the immutable Jira ticket,
  allowing existing ticket-scoped restart allowances to reach review repair
  limits correctly.
- Legacy PR-bound ledgers can consume an expiring, root-issued capability with
  an absolute repair-cycle ceiling for that repository and PR.
- The grant preserves the escalation record, blocking findings, gate history,
  prior repairs, and PR binding.
- Effective authority is queried from the root-owned host helper on every
  decision. Repository ledger edits cannot manufacture repair permission, and
  expiry or revocation restores the escalation stop.

The release does not authorize merges, erase review findings, or bypass ticket,
budget, provider, CI, or product-decision controls.
