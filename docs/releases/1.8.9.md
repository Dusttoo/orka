# Orka 1.8.9

Orka 1.8.9 replaces the durable supervisor's multi-lane global finish-first
pause with starvation-bounded fair lane allocation.

## Added

- Durable repair, recovery, continuation, dependency-unlocking, and fresh queue
  classes.
- A reserved dependency-unlocking lane whenever multi-lane capacity and an
  eligible prerequisite are both available.
- A persisted weighted cursor that favors repair and recovery without starving
  continuation or fresh work.
- Status and history evidence explaining every selected lane, queue class,
  action, and reason.
- Deterministic one-, two-, three-, and N-lane regression coverage.

## Preserved safeguards

Single-lane repositories remain strictly finish-first. Multi-lane selection
does not bypass unfinished-PR limits, dependency checks, typed resource claims,
heavy-process capacity, provider health, ticket or sprint budgets, attempt
limits, review gates, or merge authority. Skipped candidates remain queued.
