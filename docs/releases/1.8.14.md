# Orka 1.8.14

Orka 1.8.14 delivers the contract slice of ticket #75.

## Breaker taxonomy

- Adds the machine-readable `orka.breaker-classification/v1` contract.
- Inventories every current budget, run-count, review, timeout, provider,
  capacity, dependency, and integrity stop.
- Classifies stops independently by ticket/route/sprint scope and soft/hard
  strength.
- Defines durable state, wake evidence, and authority for every class.
- Maps every current controller ticket state without mutating old checkpoints.

## Safety validation

- Rejects missing, extra, duplicated, or undefined stop mappings.
- Prevents ticket and route breakers from claiming global transitions.
- Prevents hard budgets, review/security/merge gates, destructive actions,
  permanent failures, route authentication, preflight, lease, and durable-state
  controls from being softened or moved to another scope.

This release changes no runtime transition. The subsequent #75 slices enforce
the contract in ticket/route runtime behavior, global sprint admission, and
checkpoint/status integration.
