# Orka 1.8.11

Orka 1.8.11 makes the durable supervisor automatically recover an exact,
mechanically proven-dead worker attempt.

## Added

- Contract-backed stopped-attempt observation for isolated and explicitly
  opted-in cooperative execution profiles.
- Stable recovery verdicts and reason codes in controller plans.
- Transactional revalidation under the checkpoint lock before automatic
  requeue.
- Recovery audit entries binding evidence and preservation digests.
- Regression coverage for death before launch, attached worker death, verified
  progress preservation, replay, and unsettled provider work.

## Safety

Automatic recovery requires an authenticated terminal receipt, exact attempt
and invocation binding, absent descendants, and settled provider accounting.
It preserves attempt, spend, progress, branch, worktree, PR, findings, and
review-generation history. Open reservations cannot be overridden by an
operator recovery token. Existing attempt, budget, review, security, and merge
limits remain unchanged.

Preserved PRs without a bound execution attempt are not included in this
release and continue to use their existing recovery authority.
