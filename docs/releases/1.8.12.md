# Orka 1.8.12

Orka 1.8.12 lets the durable supervisor automatically reconcile an exact,
quiescent preserved PR and resume its existing continuation.

## Added

- Contract-backed preserved-PR observation across execution identity, provider
  settlement, GitHub, Git, worktree ownership, and review history.
- Stable plan verdicts for clean, dirty, active, stale, unknown-owner, and
  unsettled-reservation cases.
- Deterministic supervisor consumption of eligible `pr_reconciliation` work,
  followed by a fresh controller plan before lane allocation.
- Recovery bindings that retain the exact PR, branch, head, tree, worktree,
  attempt, invocation, eligibility digest, and preservation digest.

## Safety

The controller repeats every material observation under its checkpoint lock and
creates a usage-ledger recovery fence before changing ticket state. A moved PR
head, changed worktree, live or unknown owner, dirty tree, mismatched revision,
or open provider reservation fails closed. Reconciliation does not create a new
PR, branch, worktree, attempt history, review generation, budget grant, or merge
authority.
