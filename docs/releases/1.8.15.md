# Orka 1.8.15

Orka 1.8.15 delivers the ticket and route runtime slice of issue #75.

## Contract-driven transitions

- Binds worker timeout, recovery, decomposition, external-wait, operator, and
  irrecoverable outcomes to the canonical breaker contract.
- Publishes deterministic ticket breaker records in controller plans and
  supervisor status without changing unrelated ticket state.
- Binds provider holds to an exact route identity and role so healthy routes
  remain eligible.
- Requires a matching ticket retry record as well as the durable deadline
  before waking a stopped attempt.
- Preserves attempts, spend, findings, PR/worktree bindings, and decision
  history across parking and replay.

## Safety and verification

- Unknown sources, target-state drift, missing route identities, and tampered
  retry bindings fail closed.
- Skipped and held work consumes no new lane, attempt, or reservation.
- Regression coverage exercises desktop and API terminal delivery, ticket-only
  recovery, exact retry wakeups, route-local provider holds, planning replay,
  and status propagation.

Sprint pressure and hard global breaker transitions remain a separate issue
slice; this release does not broaden their authority.
