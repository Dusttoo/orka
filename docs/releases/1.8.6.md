# Orka 1.8.6

Orka 1.8.6 makes ticket-local parking actionable in the detached supervisor
without weakening existing controller, budget, review, or merge authority.

## Added

- No-progress timeouts enter a durable retry wait and wake at a configured
  deadline without consuming a worker lane.
- Controller-authorized repair and recovery continuations return to the launch
  queue while unrelated work remains schedulable.
- Authenticated Jira dependency changes wake only the exact externally parked
  ticket that named those dependency keys.
- The supervisor accepts bounded, contract-classified operator decision
  receipts for one ticket at a time. Legacy and scoping decisions retain their
  existing repository-policy workflows.
- Status output separates active, queued, retrying, parked, blocked, and
  terminal work.

## Safety

Workers cannot invent dependency relationships or decision classes. External
wakeups require fresh Jira evidence and a stopped execution unit. Decision
resolution requires the original classified hold, an exact class match, and a
stopped-worker proof. Ticket history, priority, dependencies, branch, worktree,
PR, review ledger, spend, and attempt history remain preserved.
