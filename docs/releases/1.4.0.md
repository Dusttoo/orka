# Orka 1.4.0 — finish-first sprint throughput

Orka now distinguishes an inactive worker from a productive long-running worker.
Local lanes have separate inactivity and total-lifetime limits, and a timed-out
attempt can continue without consuming crash-relaunch allowance only when that
exact attempt and invocation recorded new, mechanically verified progress.
Continuation allowances are capped separately; work past that cap consumes a
normal charged attempt rather than silently extending the allowance.

Sprint planning prioritizes actionable repair and recovery work over fresh
implementation and bounds unfinished PRs with `max_unmerged_prs`. Reports expose
the pipeline from attempted tickets through PR, CI, and completion, and state
explicitly that desktop subscription usage is outside the API spend ledger.
Pending continuations remain launchable at the unfinished-PR ceiling, while
direct reservations cannot bypass the controller's finish-first launch plan.

Automatic decomposition accepts a structurally valid `decompose` verdict without
reapplying the scoper's numeric threshold. Jira subtasks are decomposed into
verified sibling slices under their existing parent because Jira does not support
nested subtasks. Reuse and binding require exact type, parent, content, and
content-addressed provenance rather than trusting a predictable label alone.
Fresh authoritative Jira readiness can also release untouched
legacy holds while preserving every ticket with execution or scope evidence.
