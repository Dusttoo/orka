# Orka 1.8.10

Orka 1.8.10 defines the shared eligibility contract for automatic dead-worker
and preserved-PR recovery.

## Added

- Versioned `orka.recovery-eligibility/v1` machine-readable contract.
- A pure evaluator returning `eligible`, `waiting`, or `operator_action` with
  stable reason codes and an evidence digest.
- Distinct isolated and cooperative execution trust requirements.
- Explicit provider-settlement, work-binding, worktree, revision, and history
  preservation requirements.
- Repository-agnostic fixtures for eligible, live, unknown, pending,
  cooperative, dirty/stale, and incomplete-history cases.

## Compatibility

This release does not automatically mutate or relaunch recovered work. Existing
controller authority and recovery behavior remain unchanged while later #74
slices adopt the contract. Eligibility never grants budget, attempt, review,
repair, security, or merge authority.
